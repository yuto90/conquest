Command your islands. Capture enemy territory. Outsmart the CPU.

Conquest Isles is a single-player real-time strategy game with simple tap controls. Send troops between islands, reinforce your front line, and time your attacks to conquer the battlefield.

SIMPLE CONTROLS, TACTICAL CHOICES
Tap one of your islands, then a destination to dispatch half its troops. Your forces grow over time: decide when to defend, reinforce, or attack. Eliminate the opposing faction's islands and moving forces to win.

A NEW BATTLEFIELD EVERY MATCH
Choose from 8 to 16 islands and play on randomized maps. Try a different approach with a rematch of the same starting map and settings, or generate a new map for a fresh challenge.

LEARN, THEN RAISE THE CHALLENGE
Start with a four-step, hands-on tutorial. Choose from four CPU difficulty levels, from Very Easy to Hard, as you develop your tactics.

EARN XP AND RISE THROUGH THE RANKS
Win CPU battles to earn experience and advance your rank. The results screen shows your match summary and XP progress.

WATCH THE CPU BATTLE
Set each side's difficulty independently in CPU-versus-CPU spectator mode. Watch attacks and reinforcements unfold without taking control.

PLAY ON YOUR TERMS
• Pause and resume during a match.
• Play offline without creating an account.
• No ads or in-app purchases.
• English and Japanese interface options.
• Designed for iPhone and iPad, with portrait and landscape play on iPad.

One tap sends your troops. Your next decision shapes the battle.
